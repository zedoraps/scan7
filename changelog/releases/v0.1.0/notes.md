Scan7 keeps score for Flip 7: point the camera at a player's cards and your phone adds them up, offline. This first release covers whole games for up to 10 players in English and German, with shared games across phones as a beta.

## 🚀 Features

### Initial release

First public version of Scan7: scan each player's Flip 7 cards with the phone camera and keep score, offline, in English and German. Playing one game on several phones is included as a beta.
