This update keeps phones in a shared game connected while you scan, makes screen changes calmer and the app smaller. Sharing a game now also shows its notification on newer Android versions.

## 🔧 Changes

### Sharing a game shows its notification on Android 13 and newer

When you share or join a game, Scan7 now also asks to show notifications, so the ongoing "sharing a game" notification is visible. Saying no doesn't stop you from playing together.

### Smaller app

Scan7 is now about 6 MB smaller to download and install.

### Smoother screen changes

Screens now change with a short, quiet fade: the old screen fades out, then the new one fades in. Going back, with the button or a swipe, looks the same.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

### Joined phones no longer reconnect after every scan

In a shared game, a joined phone opened a new connection to the host about every scan and the host then dropped the old one, so scans could wait for the reconnect. A joined phone now keeps its one connection, and the host keeps one connection per phone.
