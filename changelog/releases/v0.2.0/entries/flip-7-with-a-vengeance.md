New game lets you pick Flip 7: With a Vengeance (German edition: Voll fies!). Scan each player's cards with the camera, as in Flip 7, or type them in (0–13, Lucky 13, ÷2 and the minus cards). Scan7 scores them with the Vengeance rules: ÷2 first (rounded down), then the minus cards, never below 0, then the Flip 7 bonus. A Lucky 13 lets you keep a second 13, and The Zero makes the round score 0 unless you flip 7. The score line shows each step, for example "The Zero → 0" or "− 10 → 0 + 15 Flip 7".
