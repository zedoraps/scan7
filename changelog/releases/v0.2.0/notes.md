Scan7 now counts Flip 7: With a Vengeance too: pick it under New game, then scan or type in the cards, and Scan7 scores them with the Vengeance rules. The camera now shows the whole photo it reads and leaves other players' cards at the edge out, and shared games are easier to join and to stop.

## 🚀 Features

### Flip 7: With a Vengeance

New game lets you pick Flip 7: With a Vengeance (German edition: Voll fies!). Scan each player's cards with the camera, as in Flip 7, or type them in (0–13, Lucky 13, ÷2 and the minus cards). Scan7 scores them with the Vengeance rules: ÷2 first (rounded down), then the minus cards, never below 0, then the Flip 7 bonus. A Lucky 13 lets you keep a second 13, and The Zero makes the round score 0 unless you flip 7. The score line shows each step, for example "The Zero → 0" or "− 10 → 0 + 15 Flip 7".

### Other players' cards are left out automatically

Cards from other players at the edge of the photo are now left out of the score automatically. Tap one to bring it back.

### Stop sharing from the notification

While a game is shared, the notification now says how many phones are connected (or which phone you joined) and has a Stop sharing / Leave game button, so you can end it without opening the app.

## 🔧 Changes

### After a score, the app goes back to the table

On a new install, saving a score now returns to the table instead of opening the next player's camera. To keep scanning player after player, pick “Next player” with the ▾ next to OK or under “After a score” in the round menu; the app remembers your choice.

### The camera shows the whole photo

The camera preview now shows the whole photo the app reads, not a zoomed-in crop, so you see exactly which cards get scanned.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

### Joining a shared game says what's wrong

When a phone can't join a shared game, the Join screen now says why and shows the button to fix it: Location switched off (Android needs it to find nearby phones; Scan7 never stores or shares your location), a missing permission, Google Play services refusing although the permission is granted, or the host's phone not found yet. Bluetooth or Wi-Fi switched off shows a hint, and the phone keeps trying. It no longer flickers between "Connecting" and "permissions" and no longer shows "Connecting…" forever. Tap "Technical details" on the Join screen or the share sheet to see what the radio did, and copy the log to send it along. The log stays on your phone.
