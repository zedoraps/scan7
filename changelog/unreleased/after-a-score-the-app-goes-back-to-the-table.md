---
title: After a score, the app goes back to the table
type: change
created: 2026-10-20T08:33:42.322407Z
---

On a new install, saving a score now returns to the table instead of opening the next player's camera. To keep scanning player after player, pick “Next player” with the ▾ next to OK or under “After a score” in the round menu; the app remembers your choice.
