---
title: Card scanning with the phone camera
type: feature
created: 2026-10-19T09:38:13.476921Z
---

Point the camera at one player's row of cards and tap the shutter: Scan7 finds every card and adds up the score. Detection runs on the phone, so it works offline. Zoom (0.5× to 5×, where the phone has the lens) and the torch help in dim light.
