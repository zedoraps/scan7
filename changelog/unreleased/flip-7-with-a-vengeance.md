New game lets you pick Flip 7: With a Vengeance. Type in each player's cards (0–13, Lucky 13, ÷2 and the minus cards), and Scan7 adds them up with the Vengeance rules: ÷2 first, then the minus cards, a Lucky 13 lets you keep a second 13, and The Zero makes the round score 0 unless you flip 7. Scanning these cards with the camera comes later.
