New game lets you pick Flip 7: With a Vengeance. Type in each player's cards (0–13, Lucky 13, ÷2 and the minus cards), and Scan7 adds them up with the Vengeance rules: ÷2 first, then the minus cards, a Lucky 13 lets you keep a second 13, and The Zero makes the round score 0 unless you flip 7. Scan the cards with the camera like in Flip 7 (Vengeance has its own card detector), or type them in. The score line shows each step: "The Zero → 0" when The Zero wipes the round, and "→ 0" where the minus cards stop at 0 before the Flip 7 bonus is added.
