---
title: Joining a shared game says what's wrong
type: bugfix
created: 2026-10-20T09:28:32.406285Z
---

When a phone can't join a shared game, the Join screen now says why and shows the button to fix it: Location or Bluetooth switched off, a missing permission, Google Play services refusing although the permission is granted, or the host's phone not found yet. It no longer flickers between "Connecting" and "permissions" and no longer shows "Connecting…" forever. Tap "Technical details" on the Join screen or the share sheet to see what the radio did, and copy the log to send it along. The log stays on your phone.
