---
title: Rounds, bust and the end of the game
type: feature
created: 2026-10-19T09:38:13.007237Z
---

Mark a player as bust with one tap and undo it if you tapped by mistake. Bust is always shown in magenta together with the word, never by colour alone. When a full round is scored and someone has reached the target (200 by default), the game ends and the highest total wins.
