---
title: Shared games that reconnect
type: feature
created: 2026-10-19T09:38:11.087888Z
---

Shared games reconnect by themselves after an app restart or when a phone drops out for a moment, and Join again brings a phone back into the game. A phone that joins another game pauses its own sharing and keeps its QR code. Permissions you declined can be granted later from the share sheet or the Join screen.
