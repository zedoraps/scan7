## Full description
```
Scan7 keeps score for the card game Flip 7. Point your phone at a player's row of cards, press the shutter, and Scan7 recognises the cards and adds up the points – number cards, modifiers, x2 and the Flip 7 bonus included. Check the result, fix a card if needed, save, and move on to the next player.

FAST AT THE TABLE
• One scan per player: the camera recognises the cards on your phone in a fraction of a second.
• "Scan next" goes straight to the next player's camera, so a whole round is scored in a minute.
• Bust with one tap (with Undo), or enter cards by hand when the light is bad.

THE WHOLE GAME
• Save your regular players with their own avatars and pick who plays tonight.
• Seat the players the way you sit around the table; the table view shows everyone's round.
• Running totals, the leader, the target score (200 by default) and the end of the game are handled for you.
• Scoreboard with every round; fix a past result any time.
• Past games stay on Home: winner, points, rounds.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Card recognition runs entirely on your phone. No photo ever leaves the device.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no internet access.
• Your players and games stay on your phone (and in your Android backup, if you turned it on).

OPEN SOURCE
Scan7 is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. The source code, including the card detector's training pipeline, is public; the link is in the app under About & licenses.

Scan7 is an unofficial score keeper for Flip 7. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by The Op Games. Flip 7 is a trademark of its owner and is used here only to describe what the app is for. You need your own copy of the game to play.
```
